# Rejections log

Append-only. One line per lesson: **what** was rejected and **why**. Read on
every run; never generate this kind of content again unless explicitly asked.

Format:

```
- <kind of content> — <why it was rejected> (source: <deck or date>)
```

---

- Multi-element `===` answer lists for advantages, conditions, and parameter
  sets — one card that fails inconsistently; convert to cued singles or cloze
  (source: audit of DB II decks 05/09/10, 2026-08-07)
- Symbol ↔ full definition as a `<>` two-way card — the reverse direction is a
  giveaway (source: Notation der Relationenalgebra, 2026-08-07)
- Etymology and naming trivia (`Wofür steht das B?`) — no testable content
  unless it prevents a misconception (source: deck 05, 2026-08-07)
- Tautological `Warum`-cards whose answer restates the prompt (source: deck 05,
  2026-08-07)
- Trailing "in der Praxis…" clauses appended to an answer — never tested, so
  either their own card or cut (source: deck 05, 2026-08-07)
- Definition A + definition B + `Unterschied A und B` for twins whose definition
  *is* the contrast (KNF/DNF, Operatorbaum/Zugriffsplan, lexikalisch/syntaktisch)
  — three cards over one fact; keep the contrast card alone (source: deck 10 v2,
  2026-08-07)
- Deck title or lecture title as a root bullet — nests everything one level and
  carries no recall load (source: deck 10 v2, 2026-08-07)
- Cloze pass over a symmetric equivalence rule (`KommJoin`) — no decisive term
  to hide (source: deck 10 v2, 2026-08-07)
